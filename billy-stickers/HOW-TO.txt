Billy stickers — how to use on iPhone
=====================================

Two sets:
  Midnight Gold  — 8 navy-and-gold reaction GIFs
  Swirl Ink      — 6 black-and-white swirl-eye GIFs

1. Tap & hold any sticker to Save to Photos.
2. Open Messages, or tap & hold in Photos → Add Sticker.

You can also use the GIF (and PNG) buttons, or the Download all ZIP.
