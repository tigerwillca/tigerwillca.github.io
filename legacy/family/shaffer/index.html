Born in Greeley, buried at Fort Logan

5 June 1896

Official

William Luman Shaffer was born 5 June 1896 in Greeley, Colorado, and died there on 14 April 1986, at age 89. He is buried at Fort Logan National Cemetery in Denver, Section U Site 3002, with his wife Helen.

Greeley Tribune: Command Pilot wings, 23 June 1943

23 June 1943

Confirmed

The Greeley Daily Tribune, 23 June 1943: “Capt. Shaffer Awarded Rare Pilot’s Honor.” It says Captain William L. Shaffer received Command Pilot wings, had flown well over 7,000 hours, had been an instructor at Kelly Field, and had flown the San Diego–Salt Lake City route for seven years. It also names his son, 1st Lt. William L. Shaffer Jr., then a flying instructor at Stockton, California, and his parents, Mr. and Mrs. W. D. Shaffer of 1218 Sixth Street.

Son of William L. Shaffer and Dorothy L. Hereford

22 September 1921

Official

Social Security records name William Luman Shaffer Jr. as the son of William L. Shaffer and Dorothy L. Hereford. He was born in Greeley, Colorado, on 22 September 1921 (his Social Security application; the later death index says 23 September) and died on 10 September 1995. His last residence was in ZIP 91750, La Verne, California. Dorothy Hereford was the first wife of William Luman Shaffer Sr.
