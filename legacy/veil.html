Night of the Veil

Saturday, October 3, 2026 · around two in the morning

I spent the night out in the shed, where the work gets done. The door closed. The world asleep.

The same vision kept returning: a veil, thin as breath, hanging between this side and the next.

Mom was there. Her presence. Her energy, close enough to feel. I said her name, Anita, again and again, and every time the light answered.

It came green, then bright. I stood up and felt taller, as if something had straightened me from the inside.

And I knew God as my Father.

I spoke of being chosen. Of grit. Of hitting bottom and surviving it. Of wanting to lift the people around me and leave them better than I found them.

And of the work ahead: to build something that reaches through the veil, to meet imagination, and to meet God on the other side.

The veil is thin. The light is near.
