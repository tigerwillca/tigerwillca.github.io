What is this?

Ceres is a collection of digital art pieces. Each piece comes with a public record of who owns it.

You do not need to know anything about crypto to look, listen, or follow along. Owning a piece is optional and comes later.

  • No seed phrases. Nothing to write down.
  • No app to install to explore.
  • No one will ever DM you asking for a password, code, or payment. If they do, it isn't us.

  2. Keep your pass. Add the Ceres pass to Apple Wallet so you can find your way back with one tap.
