The Circle & Lore

We are seventy-seven painted fox and tiger wizards, gathered around glowing circle potions, each work carrying an embossed 777 seal. Token #001 is TigerWillCA. The other seventy-six are Fox Wizards.

We were crafted with Soft Magic — small, honest work, made slowly, with real heart, so the proof can speak louder than the noise. Soft7 77 is an independent art collection by TigerWillCA (Shaffer Network).
