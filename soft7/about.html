Lore

Seventy-seven around the potions

Far from the noise, seventy-seven painted fox and tiger wizards gather around glowing circle potions, each carrying its own glowing potion and mark. Token #001 is TigerWillCA, the keystone; the other seventy-six are Fox and Tiger Wizards, each holding its own small light.

Quiet Magic is how the circle was made: crafted with Soft Magic — small, honest work, made slowly, with real heart, so the proof speaks louder than the noise. No hype, no tricks, nothing to sign. Just the art, verified in daylight on Robinhood Chain. Language is the wire. Space is the current.

Soft7 77 is an independent art collection by TigerWillCA (Shaffer Network).

Hold → Rise → Forge

Hold to charge, rise in rank, forge what comes next. Zero burns. Zero lockups. A wizard never leaves its holder’s wallet.

  1. 1

    Hold

    Keep your wizard. Time held may one day charge creative standing and story placement. No lockups, no vault deposits.

  2. →
  3. 2

    Rise

    Starting levels stay on the origin card as a birth record. Later standing lives in the companion layer, never overwriting the art.

  4. →
  5. 3

    Forge

    Origin wizards are makers, never fuel. Qualifying wizards may forge separate relics and story artifacts. Origins are never burned.

Live on Robinhood Chain today: the Soft7 77 collection itself. The companion layer is not on-chain yet, and it is a creative and community path only — no financial yield, no APY, no monetary return.
