# Soft7 77 Easter eggs

Not linked from the site. Paths: `/soft7/` (front) and `/soft7/about.html` (The Circle, the back page). The sequence is remembered for the browser tab.

Sound never starts by itself. Use **Sound off / Sound on**. Tones play only after that, and only from a click, tap, or hover.

## Tones

`tones/tones.json` and `tones/wav/`. Base 16 Hz. Piece `n` is harmonic `15 + n`, so 001 is 256 Hz and 077 is 1472 Hz. Batches of 11: Threshold, Wire, Current, Paint, Lantern, Span, Gate. The batch root is the first piece in the eleven. Hover or tap a piece (the 001–007 forks, or a portrait in the row of seven) to hear it. The banner pulse follows that piece's batch root.

## Sequence

1. **Seven in order.** On the fork row, play 001, then 002, then 003, then 004, then 005, then 006, then 007. A wrong piece resets. The tiger's eyes blink once in the paint, then sink back. Whisper: "Soft7 is the first door."
   Alternate: click, tap, or press Enter/Space on the living ring seven times. A gap longer than 20 seconds resets the taps. Same blink and whisper.
2. **3, 6, 9.** After the door is open, play pieces 003, 006, and 009 in that order, or press the keys 3, then 6, then 9 (not while typing in the address field). 009 is the first portrait in the row of seven. A heartbeat and a door appear. Whisper: "Pulse is the heartbeat."
3. **The span.** Click "A span of light", or press X. A bridge, one unnamed shape, one glint. Whisper: "Project X is the bridge." No logo and no mint.
4. **The horizon.** Scroll to the bottom of the page. A distant rust-colored horizon rises. It is not labeled on the page. Whisper for assistive tech: "A distant rust-colored horizon."
5. **The forks answer.** About a second later the tones of pieces 003, 006, 009, and 007 play, if sound is on. The bars glow either way. Whisper: "Enjoy the breadcrumbs, they savor better." The console prints: `Find the code. It finds you back.` A small 11:11 shows in the banner. It is not a countdown.

## After that

- Hover or focus "Soft Seven opens the gate" for about a second, or press G. "The gate was always open."
- Type `opal`. Whisper: `within the Metatron cube, they are the cube.` A slow opal wash, not a flash.

Holders (`/soft7/holders/#forks`) can play 001–007 and the seven batch roots. It does not run this sequence. The Tone trait file in `metadata/` is staged only.
