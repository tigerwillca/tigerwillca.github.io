What a square is, and why cubes do not count

Seven green #CCFF00 squares

A green #CCFF00 square is a Hood Street founding membership NFT — a neon-green square token on Robinhood Chain, also called Proof of Neon. Each square is also a token-bound wallet. It is not a Soft7 card. The original mint is closed. Squares can be found on the official Hood Street page and on OpenSea.

or

CCFF00 cubes (excluded, do not count)

A cube is a CCFF00 Cube NFT from a separate Square Ape Relic collection aligned with Hood Street (on-chain name CCFF00 Cubes, symbol CUBE). It is not a Soft7 card. The original mint is closed. Cubes can be found on OpenSea.

or

Only Squares count; cubes are excluded. Floor prices from older inventories are omitted on purpose.

FAQ

Reynard Soft7 is seven cards on Robinhood Chain. Soft7 is the first door. Pulse is the heartbeat. Project X is the bridge. You do not need to know crypto to enjoy the story. Start with the cards on the official Soft7 site.

Can I mint a Soft7 card?

No. The Soft7 collection is complete. Supply is 7, and all seven are already minted. This whitelist is not a mint of more Soft7 cards. The mint button on this page is disabled.

What does being on the list get me?

Recognition now, and first in line for what comes next. The exact grant, including any later drop, price, or supply, is To be announced.

Do cubes count?

No. Only 7 or more green #CCFF00 Squares in one wallet count. Cubes are excluded.

When does the list open, and when is the launch?

To be announced. The rule is: whitelist first, then the public launch.
