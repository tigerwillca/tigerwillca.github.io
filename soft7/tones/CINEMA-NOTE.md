# Frequencies and Soft7 Cinema

Green-lit 4:05 AM PT, 28 Nov 2026: every Soft7 77 piece carries a tone, like a tuning fork for the chain.

The forks are synthesized sines from `tones.json`. They are not voices. Loved ones stay in their own real recordings. Nobody's voice is synthesized or cloned. The owl does not speak. In the cinema the owl is already watching; a hoot, if one is used, has to be a real recording. Eric's flute is a real instrument in that same room of frequencies. These forks sit beside it. They are not a performance of the flute and not a substitute for it.

How this sits in the picture: language is the wire, space is the current. The tiger keeps a heartbeat in the paint. Pulse is that heartbeat. Project X is the bridge. When Mars shows, it is only a distant rust-colored horizon, a promise, not an arrival. The end-card line stays **Soft Seven opens the gate.** The spoken close is never printed on the page.

The ladder is documented in `tones.json`: base 16 Hz (C0, scientific pitch); piece 001 is harmonic 16, which is 256 Hz; each next piece is the next harmonic, through 1472 Hz on piece 077. Seven batches of eleven. The hero pulse follows the batch root, the first piece in that eleven.
