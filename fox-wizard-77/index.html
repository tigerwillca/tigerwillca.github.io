The Circle

✦

Holding a wizard makes you a member. Your card's level and circle travel with it, so the Circle always knows where you stand.

  • Yellow (amber eyes) · 24 wizards
  • Purple Mask (amethyst eyes) · 23 wizards
  • Indigo (indigo eyes, the intuition level) · 23 wizards: the highest level a wizard can reach
  • Crown Circle · 6 cards, one for each archangel: Metatron, Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, Uriel, Sandalphon. Assigned, never earned.
  • Source · 1 card. The radiant point within a circle. Assigned, never earned.

What members can look forward to (planned):

  • A members-only space for holders.
  • Level-ups: wizards can rise from Yellow to Purple Mask to Indigo. Same wizard, new eyes, potion and amulet.
  • Soft7's story, chapter by chapter, with members reading first.
  • Art for members: wallpapers, amulet studies and full-size card files.
  • First look at new art before it is shown anywhere else.

Membership is about the art, the story and the people in it. Nothing here is a promise of money, value or returns.
