Bark Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026
Publisher: Schaefer Enterprises / William Shaffer Jr.

Bark is an iPhone app that plays dog sounds and helps you talk with your dog. Bark works offline. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Bark does not send any of your data to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. Bark keeps a small amount of information on your iPhone so the app works:

  • Your settings: the breed and mood you pick, and whether Talk is turned on.
  • Recordings of your dog (optional): if you tap Record, Bark saves your dog's bark as an audio file on your iPhone.
  • Training labels (optional): if you teach Bark your dog's barks, it saves your labels plus a few numbers that describe each sound (like pitch, length, and loudness) on your iPhone. It does not keep the audio for these.

Microphone and speech recognition

  • Microphone: Bark uses the microphone only when you tap Record, hold Talk, or tap Listen. Listening for barks is done on your iPhone.
  • Speech recognition (Hold to Talk): Bark uses Apple's speech recognition set to on-device only. Your words are recognized on your iPhone and are never sent to Apple's servers or to us. If your iPhone can't recognize speech on-device, Talk stays off; there is no online fallback. The words are used only to pick a matching dog sound and are not saved.

How it's used

Everything above is used only to run Bark's features on your iPhone: playing the right sounds, playing your own dog's recordings, and matching barks. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

Storage and deletion

All data stays on your iPhone, inside Bark's own storage. You can delete individual recordings or reset training in the app. Deleting the Bark app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Sharing

None. Bark does not share, sell, or send your data to anyone, including us.

Children

Bark does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
