THE MOM SEQUENCE - real photos go here (optional). Note: this folder is published on the web with the page, so any photo added here becomes public.

Put real photos of Mom in this "media" folder, next to index.html,
using exactly these names. Any you don't have can simply be left out; that age
then shows her light only.

  mom-16.jpg   Mom at about sixteen
  mom-21.jpg   Mom at twenty-one (the year Will was born)
  mom-30.jpg   Mom in her thirties
  mom-40.jpg   Mom in her forties
  mom-50.jpg   Mom in her fifties
  mom-60.jpg   Mom at sixty

Tips:
- Face roughly centered; each photo is shown softly inside her light in a circle,
  so a square-ish crop works best.
- iPhone photos: export/share as JPG ("Most Compatible") if they're HEIC.
- Different names or paths can be set in the CONFIG block at the top of
  index.html (CONFIG.PHOTOS).

Words:
- Will's apology is in CONFIG.APOLOGY (his words).
- CONFIG.MOM_MESSAGE is left empty on purpose. It's only for what Will himself
  writes ("I want her to tell me..."). Until then a single blossom drifts where
  it will appear. Nothing is ever written for Mom.

No voice or face is ever generated or imitated. The microphone (optional) is only
used live to brighten Will's light; nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
